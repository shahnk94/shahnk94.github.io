About — shahriar.sys

Project Driver. Governance Guardian. AI Adopter.

I work the layer underneath the search box. What a document is called, who owns it, how long it lives, who's allowed to see it, and whether a person or an AI assistant finds the right version on the first try. That's the day job at PCL Construction in Edmonton: enterprise content governance and information architecture, the BI reporting leadership reads, and the change management that decides whether any of it gets used. Structure on its own convinces nobody.

Before that, Senior Technology Project & Business Development Lead at Middle East Water & Environment. Ten workstreams, three delivery partners, procurement running across five-plus concurrent projects, and a water treatment plant rated at 250,000 cubic metres a day whose SCADA readings, lab results and compliance records all described the same asset. I built the common data environment on SharePoint and Dynamics 365 that all three partners worked inside, so a drawing had one address across all of them. Change orders came down 25%.

Fifty-plus projects to date, across construction, water infrastructure, engineering consulting, a university CIO office and my own e-commerce startup — in Canada and the Middle East. Some of those never became projects, which was the point. That's the feasibility pass: whether the thing should exist at all, answered on paper before money moves, with a short list of options held against doing nothing and a rough order of magnitude a sponsor can push on rather than a recommendation dressed as certainty.

How I start

Find out which system is the source of record for each number, and who actually owns it. Sit with the people who have to live in the thing. Get one definition agreed per figure before anyone builds a report on it. Then pick the smallest change that makes the next decision easier, and ship that first.

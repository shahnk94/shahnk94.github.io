Case Files

Six cases. Situation, what I owned, what I did, what came out.

Membrane Manufacturing Plant: feasibility to award recommendation
Feasibility & Tender Lead · MEWE, for owner Asia Water Co. · Bandar Abbas · 2024-2025 · $35M estimated CAPEX
Situation

Asia Water Co. was weighing a membrane manufacturing plant at roughly $35M CAPEX. No feasibility case existed, and no procurement route had been chosen.

What I owned

The pre-investment case and the route to market: documented ground for a $35M commitment, ending in one award recommendation.

What I did

Ran the feasibility study and built the CAPEX/OPEX model the investment decision rested on. Researched vendors and procurement methods. Structured the work as a two-package RFP tender, wrote the packages, then ran the evaluation that took 11 bidders down to a shortlist of 4.

Result

A $33M award recommendation delivered, and an owner able to sign against a documented CAPEX/OPEX model.

PCL Connects 5.0: one information architecture across 30+ teams
Project & Governance Lead · PCL Construction, Edmonton · 2025 to now · $1M+ annual spend · 13,000+ documents
Situation

A Microsoft 365 modernization covering 13,000+ documents and 3,000+ pages across 25 business areas, with each team structuring content its own way.

What I owned

One information architecture across 30+ teams, and the governance that makes PCL content usable by AI.

What I did

Built a 22-domain business taxonomy, six governance capability categories and a 2025-2027 roadmap. Restructured content and information-management processes against new corporate directives. Scored documents by risk instead of reviewing everything. Authored the templated guidelines teams operate from and ran the change management behind them, working decisions through 12+ subject-matter experts and product owners.

Result

Audit readiness up 40%. Review scope cut 30% across 3,000+ documents, with the escalation framework and dashboards leadership uses. The Content Governance App I made the case for is now used team-wide.

National water-research data-centre program: controls and one data environment
Senior Technology Project & Business Development Lead, PMO · MEWE, Tehran · Oct 2023 to Feb 2025 · joint IPD PMO
Situation

Sponsor, contractors and consultants delivering a national water-research data-centre program from a joint integrated project delivery PMO, each holding their own version of the record.

What I owned

The controls layer, and one environment the whole delivery chain could work from.

What I did

Wrote the charters, communication plans and RACI charts, stood up risk registers and risk matrices, and tracked dependencies across teams. Designed the structure, permissions and rules of an access-controlled SharePoint and Dynamics 365 common data environment, then turned field and office workflows into system requirements through cross-functional workshops and user acceptance testing.

Result

Change orders down 25% once the controls layer was in place. Sponsor, contractors and consultants working off one access-controlled record, with permissions set by role.

27 districts, one KPI and pipeline framework
Senior Digital Communications Specialist (Information Governance & Project Delivery) · PCL Construction · 2025 to now
Situation

Strategic-plan reporting and Salesforce pipeline data lived apart. Twenty-seven districts described performance in numbers that didn't compare, and executives decided without a shared figure.

What I owned

The measurement layer. One definition per metric, a named owner behind each number, one source executives could decide from.

What I did

Unified strategic-plan reporting and Salesforce pipeline data into a single framework, defined the KPI set and the pipeline measures under it, and settled who owns each number before anyone built a report on it. Then took those definitions through all 27 districts.

Result

One governed source of truth across 27 districts, used for executive decisions. Marketing and pursuit performance now reads the same way everywhere, against definitions with names attached.

Firm-wide SharePoint hub, governance built from zero
Business Intelligence Lead · Peyvareh Engineering & Construction, Tehran · Mar 2020 to Oct 2023 · 10+ teams
Situation

Teams on two major projects kept documents wherever they landed. No content types, no managed metadata, no retention, no agreed permissions.

What I owned

Making documents findable and keeping them that way, which meant governance first and a platform people would actually use.

What I did

Established information governance from the ground up: content types, managed metadata, permissions, retention rules. Rolled out the firm-wide SharePoint hub and trained 40+ engineers and coordinators. Alongside it, refined how scheduling and planning were reported across 10+ teams and automated Excel and Power BI over ERP data into forecasts and KPI dashboards.

Result

Document search time down nearly 50%. Support tickets down 20%. The reporting gaps that surfaced cut schedule slippage 20% on the same two projects.

PCL AI Hub: company-wide launch
Senior Digital Communications Specialist (Information Governance & Project Delivery) · PCL Construction · 2025 · with an external AI consultant
Situation

PCL wanted a company-wide AI hub. Whatever it retrieved would only be as good as the structure underneath it, and that structure wasn't settled.

What I owned

The proposal, the information architecture the hub retrieves against, and the launch date.

What I did

Proposed the hub, designed its information architecture, and tied it to the governance already in flight: the 22-domain taxonomy, the six capability categories, the 2025-2027 roadmap. Ran delivery with the external AI consultant against the date.

Result

Launched on schedule, company-wide, retrieving against the same taxonomy PCL's content is being restructured to. No separate rulebook for the AI.
